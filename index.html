About

Background

I moved from poetry to film, wrote narrative for a shipped mobile RPG, then spent my MFA building rooms, toys, and tabletop games that people play with their hands.

Find the mechanic inside the subject.Nüshu became a decoding game, shadows became a size mechanic, a fridge became a crime scene. The rules come from the material.
Let the object carry the story.Domestic objects, plush toys, cards, and capsules hold the narrative, so people find it by touching, opening, and matching.
Prototype in the real medium.ESP32, NFC, sensors, Unity, and web builds. I test with players early and change what the room tells me to change.
